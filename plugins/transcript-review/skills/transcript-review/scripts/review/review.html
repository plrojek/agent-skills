How to correct this passage
  1. Press ▶ Play (or Esc) and listen. The line being played lights up blue. Shift+Esc = 3 s back. Click a line's time to play from there.
  2. Fix the words so each line says exactly what was said. Delete words nobody said. Can't make it out? Type [?]. A note for the reader goes in square brackets, e.g. [on the phone].
  3. One line = one person. Where the next person starts talking, put the cursor there and press Enter: the rest moves to a new line. Then set who is talking: click the coloured name, or press Ctrl+1…8.
  4. Two lines that are really one person? Put the cursor at the start of the lower line and press Backspace: they join.
  5. Add a line: pause where someone starts talking and press ＋ line at current time (top), or press ＋ at the end of a line. Delete a line: 🗑. Change a line's time: type it in the time box, e.g. 01:09.5 (lines re-sort by time).
  6. When the whole passage is right, press Passage finished at the bottom. Everything saves by itself.
